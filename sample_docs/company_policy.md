# Leave Policy

Employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Unused leave can be carried forward up to 10 days. Sick leave is separate and requires a medical certificate after 3 consecutive days.

# Travel Reimbursement

Travel expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days after the claim is submitted with receipts. Flights must be booked in economy class unless the trip exceeds 8 hours.

# IT Support

The error code ERR-4021 means the payment gateway timed out. Retry after 5 minutes; if it persists, raise a ticket with the finance-systems team.
